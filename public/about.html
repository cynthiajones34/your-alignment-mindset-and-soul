Hi, I'm Christina.

I help women who feel stressed, stuck, or disconnected from themselves build a resilient mindset, nourish their bodies, and create a more peaceful, energized life from the inside out.

I'm a wife, mother of 4 beautiful children and a fur baby, and a former corporate baddie. After navigating the corporate world for 20 years, my passion for wellness and lifestyle balance led me in a different direction.

I started my coaching method with one goal in mind: to help women journey through the pillars of wellness with a guide and a friend, in a safe space for growth and restoration.

My Coaching Method: Focus. Reflect. Refine.

Every client I work with moves through the same three-phase framework, because real alignment isn't about adding more to your life. It's about getting clear on what belongs there.

01
Focus

It all starts by learning how to focus on what you want. We identify what matters most and build clarity around it.

02
Reflect

Next, you'll reflect on what may be blocking you and learn how to overcome these obstacles. This is where the real shifts happen.

03
Refine

The last step? We learn how to continually refine what we've learned. Think of this as your new beginning, not a finish line.

How we work together

I offer 1:1 virtual coaching sessions as well as community-based group sessions where you can grow alongside like-minded women on a similar journey.

Whether you're coming in burned out, stuck, or simply ready for more, there's a path here for you. And it starts with a conversation.
