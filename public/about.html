Certified Holistic Wellness Coach · Atlanta, GA

I built Y.A.M.S. because I needed it first.

There was a season in my life when I was doing everything right by every measure that society told me to care about. The career. The hustle. The showing up for everyone around me.

And I was disappearing.

Not dramatically. Slowly. The kind of disappearing that happens when you stop asking yourself what you actually want because you've been so busy managing everything else.

That season changed me. It also led me here.

I created Y.A.M.S. Wellness Your Alignment, Mindset & Soul as the space I wish I'd had. A place where high-achieving Black women could come not just to be coached, but to be seen. To slow down enough to hear themselves think. To build lives that feel as good as they look.

The YAMS Method: Focus. Reflect. Refine.

Every client I work with moves through the same three-phase framework because real alignment isn't about adding more to your life. It's about getting clear on what belongs there.

Focus

We identify the exact area creating the most friction in your life right now. We don't try to fix everything. We go deep on what matters most.

Reflect

We examine the patterns, beliefs, and stories underneath the surface the ones that have been driving the bus without your permission.

Refine

We build a new operating system. One that is actually yours grounded in your values, your energy, your vision of what a full life looks like.

My approach

I believe that rest is not a reward. That high achievement and deep peace are not opposites. That Black women deserve spaces where they don't have to perform, code-switch, or earn their own care.

I hold that belief in every session.
