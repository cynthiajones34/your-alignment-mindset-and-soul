Holistic Wellness Coach · Atlanta, GA

Hi, I'm Christina.

I'm a wife, mother of four amazing children, and proud dog mom. I was born in Germany as a military brat, and lived in several places across the US, but Atlanta is the place I now call home.

For years, I poured my energy into building a successful career, supporting my family, and showing up for everyone around me. Like so many women, I became incredibly good at taking care of others—but somewhere along the way, I realized I needed to reconnect with myself.

That realization changed everything.

I discovered that true wellness isn't about perfection, chasing the next diet, or checking another box on an already full to-do list. It's about creating a life where your mind, body, and spirit work together. It's about slowing down, honoring your needs, and choosing yourself without guilt.

That's why I created Y.A.M.S. (Your Active Mindset & Spirit).

My mission is simple: to help women stop merely surviving and begin living with intention, confidence, vitality, and peace. I want every woman I work with to know that she doesn't have to navigate life's transitions alone. Whether you're feeling burned out, disconnected, overwhelmed, or simply ready for more, this is a space where you can breathe, grow, heal, and rediscover the woman you've always been.

My coaching blends my BA in Sociology with over 15 years of background in corporate leadership, coaching, and personal development and my lifelong passion for movement, nutrition, mindfulness, and holistic wellness. Together, we'll focus on the pillars of wellness to create sustainable habits that support the life you truly want—not just the life you've become accustomed to living.

I believe wellness is a journey, not a destination. There will be moments of growth, moments of challenge, and moments of celebration. Through it all, I'll be here as your coach, your guide, and someone who genuinely believes in what's possible for you.

My Coaching Method: Focus. Reflect. Refine.

Every client I work with moves through the same three-phase framework, because real alignment isn't about adding more to your life. It's about getting clear on what belongs there.

01
Focus

It all starts by learning how to focus on what you want. We identify what matters most and build clarity around it.

02
Reflect

Next, you'll reflect on what may be blocking you and learn how to overcome these obstacles. This is where the real shifts happen.

03
Refine

The last step? We learn how to continually refine what we've learned. Think of this as your new beginning, not a finish line.

If you're ready to choose yourself, embrace your next chapter, and become the healthiest, most aligned version of you, I'd be honored to walk alongside you.

Shoulders back. Chest up. Head held high, Queen. Your journey starts here.
