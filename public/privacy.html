Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 11, 2026

Y.A.M.S. (Your Alignment, Mindset & Soul) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit this website, subscribe to our list, submit a form, or use our coaching services, and how we use, share, and protect that information. By using this website, you consent to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • ✦ Your name and email address when you subscribe to our list or download a free resource.
  • ✦ Information you submit through the contact form or a Vibe Check application, such as your name, email, and the details you share about your goals.
  • ✦ Account and session information if you become a client, including booking details, session notes, and progress you choose to record in your client portal.
  • ✦ Limited technical information collected automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, used to keep the site secure and working.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • ✦ Deliver the resources, coaching, sessions, and services you request.
  • ✦ Respond to your inquiries and schedule your sessions.
  • ✦ Send emails you have opted into, such as the free 5-Day Alignment Reset and periodic notes on rest, purpose, and alignment.
  • ✦ Process payments for services you purchase.
  • ✦ Maintain, secure, and improve the website and our services.

3. Email Communications

When you subscribe, you agree to receive emails from Y.A.M.S. You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email or by contacting us at journey@myyams.com. We do not sell or rent your email address.

4. Payment Processing

Payments for services are processed through PayPal. When you pay an invoice, your payment details are handled directly by PayPal under its own privacy policy. Y.A.M.S. does not collect or store your full card or bank account numbers.

5. Service Providers We Use

We rely on trusted third-party providers to operate this website and our services. These providers process data only to provide their services to us:

  • ✦ Google Firebase, for website hosting, database storage, and secure sign-in to the client portal.
  • ✦ Microsoft 365, for our email and scheduling.
  • ✦ PayPal, for payment processing.
  • ✦ Cloudflare, for content delivery, website security, and privacy-friendly, cookieless website analytics (aggregate page views and performance).

6. Cookies

This website uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies to keep the site secure, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies. Disabling cookies may affect how parts of the site function.

7. How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access-controlled storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only with the service providers listed above, or when required by law, to enforce our Terms, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Y.A.M.S. or others.

9. Your Rights & Choices

You may request to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you may opt out of marketing emails at any time. To make a request, contact us at journey@myyams.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

10. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we take steps to delete or de-identify it.

11. Children's Privacy

This website and our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

This website may link to third-party sites and social platforms such as Instagram and TikTok. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies.
